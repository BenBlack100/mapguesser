Map Guesser Privacy Policy

Last updated 13 November 2026

Map Guesser does not collect any personal data. There are no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking, and nothing you do in the game is sent to us.

Your progress

Your scores, streaks and settings are stored only on your device. They are not sent anywhere. Deleting the app deletes them.

Photos

Some questions and fact cards show photos from Wikimedia Commons. These are loaded from upload.wikimedia.org when they are shown, so Wikimedia receives a normal web request from your device, like any website would. See the Wikimedia Foundation privacy policy.

Game Center

If you use Game Center, your scores and achievements are handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy.

Children

Map Guesser is suitable for all ages. Because it collects no personal data, it collects none from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
