Map Guesser Support

The geography quiz for iPhone and iPad

Need help, found a mistake on a map, or have an idea? Email ben@viralview.ai. If something is wrong with a place or a fact, please include its name.

Where is my progress saved?

On your iPhone or iPad. It isn't synced between devices, and deleting the app deletes it.

How do I use leaderboards and achievements?

They use Game Center. Sign in under Settings → Game Center on your device, then open Map Guesser again.

Photos aren't showing

Photos come from Wikimedia Commons and need an internet connection. The rest of the game works offline.
